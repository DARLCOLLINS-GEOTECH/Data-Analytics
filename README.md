## 🔍 My Analytical Workflow

My general approach to data analytics projects follows the process below:

### 1. Define the Problem

Identify the main question, objective, or business problem the analysis is expected to address.

### 2. Collect and Understand the Data

Examine the dataset, variables, data sources, structure, limitations, and relevance to the analytical question.

### 3. Clean and Prepare the Data

Handle missing values, duplicates, inconsistencies, incorrect data types, formatting issues, and other data-quality problems.

### 4. Explore the Data

Use descriptive statistics, aggregation, filtering, grouping, and visualization to identify important patterns and relationships.

### 5. Analyse and Visualize

Apply appropriate analytical techniques and develop charts, dashboards, tables, or other visual representations of the data.

### 6. Generate Insights

Interpret the results and identify trends, patterns, anomalies, relationships, and key findings.

### 7. Communicate Findings

Present conclusions and recommendations in a clear, concise, and decision-focused manner.
